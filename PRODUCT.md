# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Professional CDL drivers, typically 50–65. They use the portal one-handed on a phone,
frequently outdoors in direct sunlight, often in or beside a truck rather than at a desk.
They are fluent in commercial instrumentation — ELDs, CAT scales, Garmin, Samsara,
Qualcomm, dash clusters — so gauges and telemetry read as native, not decorative.

The job: confirm membership standing at a glance, then act — check referral progress,
pull up the member card, find a terminal, reach support. Sessions are short and
interruption-prone. Presbyopia is the norm in this age band, and sunlight collapses
effective contrast, so anything small or low-contrast is functionally invisible in
the real usage context.

## Product Purpose

The Outriders Driver Portal is the membership credential for the LineHaul Station
drivers' club. It exists to make membership feel *earned and visible*, and to give
members a single place to track standing, referrals, benefits and locations.

Success: a driver opens the app and thinks "I feel like a valued member" — not
"I'm looking at another mobile app."

## Brand Personality

Exclusivity, belonging, confidence, reliability, industrial craftsmanship.

Three words: **premium, industrial, deliberate.**

The reference is an American Express Platinum card or the Centurion Lounge, rebuilt
in heavy-duty equipment materials — brushed steel, carbon fiber, machined instruments.
Every interaction should feel mechanical and intentional, never soft or playful.

## Anti-references

Explicitly not: cyberpunk, hacker interface, gaming UI, glassmorphism, neon glow,
Tesla minimalism, consumer SaaS dashboard.

Also not: generic progress bars where an instrument would read better; decorative
typography; futuristic styling. "Premium industrial, never futuristic."

## Design Principles

1. **Membership before utility.** Identity establishes itself first; tools follow.
2. **Information before decoration.** Ornament that carries no data is cut.
3. **Large numbers over long paragraphs.** Glanceable magnitude beats prose.
4. **Readable in three seconds** — in sunlight, one-handed, at arm's length.
5. **Whitespace is a premium material.** Let everything breathe.
6. **Consistency over novelty.** A known pattern beats a clever one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as the floor: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and meaningful
graphics.

**Plus a sunlight legibility floor that AA does not cover.** AA constrains contrast
but says nothing about size, and this audience is defined by presbyopia plus direct
sunlight:

- No text below **13px** at any breakpoint, for any role including captions and labels.
- Real body and supporting copy at **≥15px**.
- Numeric values that carry meaning get size and weight, not muted color.
- Never encode state in color alone — pair with icon, label, or position.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; instrument animation is enhancement, never the
  only way a value is communicated.
- Touch targets ≥44×44pt with ≥8px separation.
